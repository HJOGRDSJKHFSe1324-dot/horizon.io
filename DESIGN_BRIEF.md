# HORIZON Design Brief v3 (Overhaul)

## Concept
Quiet, premium AI workstation. Editorial rhythm over template grid. Asymmetry, deliberate whitespace, one confident accent.

## Palette
- Dark bg #0B0D12, raised #12151D, surface #1A1E28, border #262B3A
- Light bg #F6F8FC, raised #FFFFFF, surface #EEF2F8, border #DCE3EE
- Text dark #F5F7FA, muted #A3AAB9, subtle #6B7280
- Text light #0F172A, muted #475569, subtle #94A3B8
- Accent #4F7CF3, AI accent #22D3EE, success #22C55E, danger #F87171

## Type
- Display: Space Grotesk 500/600, tracking -0.02em, 40/48
- Body: Inter 400/500, line-height 1.55
- Code: JetBrains Mono 400, 0.95em
- Scale: 12/14/16/18/20/24/32/40/48

## Spacing
- 4px grid, max content 1280px, prose max 68ch
- section spacing 96/64/44
- radius: controls 6, cards 12, containers 16, pills 999

## Motion
- 100/180/240/320ms durations; enter out-quart, exit in-quart
- transform+opacity only; reduced-motion disables all

## Anti-AI rules
- No default purple-blue neon hero.
- No emoji icons.
- No repetitive 3-up card grid.
- Avoid "unleash, elevate, seamless, revolutionize, cutting-edge".
- Replace with concrete copy and varied rhythm.
- Any filler or generic copy rejected.
